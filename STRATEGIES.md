# Strategies

Strategies are routed through the registry/consensus layer and unknown sources are rejected by the trading engine.

The requested formal metadata (`strategyId`, version, market regime, parameters, risk profile, required timeframe/data, performance metrics, enabled state) is not yet represented as one complete first-class registry schema.

Promotion through research -> backtest -> validation -> walk-forward -> Monte Carlo -> paper -> testnet -> shadow -> small capital -> production is not yet enforced by executable promotion gates.
